## Setup

1. Load the debug save.
2. Dev mode on.
3. Spawn the test dragon (`Dovahkiin_Alduin_Test`) near some colonists.

## Test 1 — the after-breath hold survives its own kill ⭐ THE IMPORTANT ONE

This is the fix with the widest blast radius, and the one most likely to be wrong.

1. Let him roll a **breath** pattern (hover or perch) and fire it at a colonist.
2. **Aim for a breath that KILLS whoever it hits** — that is the case that was broken. If the
   victim survives, wait for another breath.

**PASS:** after the breath ends he stays **motionless for about 4 seconds**, keeps the **same
facing**, and only then lifts into the flight sprite.

**FAIL:** he snaps to the flight sprite while still frozen in place. That was the bug — the victim
dying pre-empted the hold.

> The old symptom, in the user's words: *"already switching to flight state while still being in
> his after-breath pause phase."*

---

## Test 2 — the circling leg always gets its 15 seconds

1. Watch him complete any attack pattern.
2. Time from the end of that attack to the start of the **next** one.

**PASS:** at least **15 seconds** of circling flight between attacks, every time — including when
the previous attack killed its target.

**FAIL:** he attacks again inside 15 seconds.

`dragonPatternLeaveTicks` is **900** (15s) and was already correct; the phase was being thrown
away, not cut short. If this still fails, the number is not the place to look.

## Test 3 — no breath while something is in his jaws ⭐

1. Set `dovahGrabChance` to **1.0** in `Defs/MiscDefs/DovahkiinTuningDef.xml` so every bite grabs.
   **⚠ PUT IT BACK TO 0.02 WHEN YOU ARE DONE.**
2. Restart, let him brawl, and watch a grab and shake happen.

**PASS:** while he is shaking a pawn he does **nothing else** — no breath, no new pattern. The
attack he was on resumes after he lets go, rather than instantly rolling the next one.

**FAIL:** anything fires during the shake.

> *"i caught him bite-shaking a pawn then finishing him with the ground-breath… should be possible?
> NO, SHOULDN'T BE POSSIBLE!!!"*

## Test 4 — facing matches direction

1. Watch a long circling leg.

**PASS:** the sprite's direction matches where he is actually going. No "flying east while facing
south-east".

**FAIL:** report which direction he was moving and which way the sprite faced — those two words are
what fixed it last time.

## Test 5 — the brawl gaze

1. Let him land in a **dive-and-brawl** and kill or drive off everyone near him.

**PASS:** while standing with nothing in reach he **turns to face the nearest enemy**, or — with
nobody at all in sight — **glances around**, a quarter turn every 1.5–2.5 seconds.

**FAIL (important):** he ends up **stuck facing one way for ever**, including after taking off.
That would mean the sprite lock is not being released, which is the known trap for this mechanism.

---

## Test 6 — stun immunity, and that it did not eat the breath hold

1. Have a colonist hit him with **Unrelenting Force** (or anything that stuns) **while he is in the
   air**.

**PASS:** he keeps flying — no freeze.

**⚠ THEN RE-CHECK TEST 1.** The post-breath hold is *itself* implemented as a stun, and a breath
happens while soaring — which counts as airborne. If Test 1 passed before this and fails after,
the self-stun exemption is the thing to look at, not the stun patch.

---

## Test 7 — speed over rough ground

1. Watch him fly over **snow, mud or rubble**.

**PASS:** no slow-down. His flight speed looks constant.

**NOT IN SCOPE:** he will still **path around walls** rather than over them. That is a known
missing feature (`SPEC.md §6.5`), it is the next piece of work, and it is not a failure of this
build.
