## Test 1 — The armour lies down with her

This is the one you reported: she went down and her armour stayed standing over her.

1. Cast **mul qah diiv** on the Dovahkiin so the level-3 armour is up.
2. Get her **downed** — dev mode **Damage** tool is fastest, or let a fight do it.
3. ✅ The **plates lie over with her body**, not standing upright above her.
4. ✅ The **helm is ON her head** — at the head end of the lying body, covering it.
5. ✅ The **fins and crest** lie with the body too, pointing the same way they do standing.

**Point 4 is the whole test.** If the helm sits *beside* her — over her chest or her hip, with her
bare head showing next to it — then the offset is being rotated wrongly and I want to know
immediately. You have the picture of both outcomes; cell 3 is right, cell 4 is the failure.

She may lie head-left or head-right; RimWorld picks by pawn ID and both are correct.

> **If a colonist rescues her into a BED, ignore what you see there.** The armour will lie at the
> right angle but may sit offset from her body. That is a **separate, older gap** — the overlay
> reads the pawn's own position while RimWorld draws a pawn in a bed at a position derived from
> the *bed*. It is not this fix failing. **Test her on open ground.** I have flagged it; it is a
> small change and it is yours to schedule.

5. Stand her back up (heal her) → ✅ the armour stands with her again.

## Test 4 — His frost breath looks like hers

His frost was firing the *force* wave's fleck. The cost was never colour — it was **timing**:
force holds 0.16s and fades over 0.45, frost holds 0.20 and fades over 0.55. His frost cleared
the air about a fifth faster than yours.

1. Have the **Dovahkiin** cast **Frost Breath**. Watch how long the wave hangs in the air.
2. Summon him and wait for **his** frost breath (he cycles Fire → Frost → Unrelenting Force).
3. ✅ His frost **hangs like yours** rather than snapping away.

**Be honest with yourself on this one — it is a fifth of a second and it is subtle.** The real
check already passed without the game: the code now names the frost fleck, that fleck exists, and
a name that did not resolve would throw a loud error at startup. If you cannot tell the
difference, that is fine and expected. What matters is that you see **no red errors** in the log.

## Test 5 — His Unrelenting Force against armour

His Force had **zero** armour penetration, so blunt armour reduced it completely — against
anything plated he did nothing while his fire and frost still landed. Now 0.35.

1. Summon him with an **armoured** enemy on the map — a Profaned Legion raider, a marine, anything
   in real plate. Not a naked tribal; that proves nothing.
2. Wait for his **Unrelenting Force** (third in his cycle — the blue shove, not a breath).
3. ✅ The armoured target is **knocked back, stunned, and takes damage**.
4. ❌ If it shrugs the hit off entirely, the penetration is still not reaching the damage.

The same failure hit Soul Tear once and read as "completely broken". A knockback with no damage
number floating off the target is the tell.

This is also tunable now: `ancientDragonbornForceArmorPenetration` in the same XML file.
