## Test 1 — he does not land or walk under a roof

**Setup:** a colony with a roofed building — a castle interior, a barracks, anything with a roof
over floor. Put two or three pawns **inside** it.

1. Dev mode → spawn `Dovahkiin_Alduin_Test` **outside**, 30-plus cells away.
2. Watch him come in and attack the pawns indoors.

**PASS:**
- he **never touches the floor inside** the roofed area
- against the indoor pawns he stays airborne and **breathes down at them** instead of landing
- if he ever is on the ground under a roof (spawn him inside on purpose to force it) he **lifts into
  a soar within a second**

**FAIL and worth reporting exactly:** he lands indoors; he stands under a roof and stays; he
flickers up-and-down repeatedly at the doorway (that would be the oscillation the precedence rule
is meant to prevent — say so and I will look at `MustBeGrounded` first).

**Also check the opposite, because I changed the landing:** with the target **outdoors**, the dive
pounce must still work as before — he drops onto a cell beside them, dust and stun included. If the
dive stopped happening outdoors, that is my roof filter being too broad.

| knob | value | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| `dragonNeverGroundedUnderRoof` | `true` | false restores the old behaviour completely |
